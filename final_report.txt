## Identified Root Cause
**Primary Issue (Fixed)**: Symmetry values were being double-normalized in risk_engine.py. The calculate_symmetry() function already returns a 0-100 score (100 = perfect symmetry), but this score was being passed through normalize_metric() again, causing perfect symmetry (0° difference) to score 0.0 instead of 100.0.

**Secondary Issues (Not Calculation Bugs)**:
- hip_angle component score remains 0.0 because measured hip_angle values (shoulder-hip-knee angle) are consistently below the hard_min threshold of 140.0°, causing them to be clamped to 140.0° and then normalized to 0.0% as they fall below the ideal range [160.0, 180.0].
- temporal_stability component score remains 0.0 because the standard deviation of knee_angle over time exceeds the hard_max threshold of 15.0°, resulting in a 0.0 score after normalization.
